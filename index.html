About The Project

Bridging the Healthcare Gap in Rural India

Sehat-Saathi is an AI-powered health companion designed specifically for rural communities across India. We understand that access to reliable healthcare information is a challenge in remote areas.

Our chatbot speaks your language—literally. Available in Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, and more, Sehat-Saathi delivers verified medical information, reminds you of vaccination schedules, and alerts you about disease outbreaks in your area.
